# Code of Conduct

We welcome respectful collaboration among molecular researchers, developers,
students and other members of the open-source community.

## Expected behavior

- Treat participants with respect regardless of background, experience,
  identity, location or professional role.
- Give constructive technical and scientific feedback; disagree with ideas,
  not individuals.
- Credit contributors and sources appropriately.
- Respect privacy, research ethics, intellectual property and applicable
  institutional data-sharing obligations.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, targeted personal attacks,
unwelcome sexual attention, publication of private information without
permission, and deliberate disruption of collaboration are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer using the contact
methods on [the maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/thanh727).
Avoid publishing sensitive details in GitHub issues.

The maintainer may warn, edit or remove problematic material, restrict
participation or block users when necessary to protect the community.
Reports will be handled as discreetly as practicable; confidentiality
cannot be absolutely guaranteed.
